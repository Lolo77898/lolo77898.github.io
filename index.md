## 🌱 How to Use orchids-api

After launching orchids-api, you can begin to categorize and manage your orchids. Here are some basic functions you can try:

- **Add an Orchid**: Click on the “Add” button to enter details about your new orchid.
- **View Orchids**: Use the "View" option to see a list of all your orchids.
- **Edit Information**: Select any entry and click “Edit” to update details.
- **Delete an Orchid**: If you need to remove an entry, use the “Delete” option.
